Cmake revolves around targets, of which there are two primary types:
    -> Libraries
        -> add_library(name, <shared, static>, imported?)
    -> Executables
        -> add_executable()


Commands revolve around modifying targets


target_sources: https://cmake.org/cmake/help/latest/command/target_sources.html
    -> File sets are just the "name" of the collection of files we are adding.
